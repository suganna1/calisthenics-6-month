    Notes & settings

    This is a beginner-start, no-equipment programme. Workouts are about 20 minutes, Monday to Friday. Saturday and Sunday are rest days. Ticks and your streak are saved on this phone only.

    How to install on iPhone

    • Open this page in Safari (not Chrome or in-app browsers).
    • Tap the Share button.
    • Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

    On Android, open the page in Chrome and use Add to Home screen when asked, or from the browser menu.

    Before you train

    If you have a health condition, an injury, chest pain, dizziness, or you have been inactive for a long time, speak to a GP before you start. Stop if something feels wrong. This page is a training plan, not medical advice.

    Warm-up

    Most of the follow-along videos include a short warm-up. If one does not, spend two or three minutes on easy arm circles, leg swings, and bodyweight squats first. Move in a range that feels comfortable.

    Food, in brief

    To lose fat you generally need to eat fewer calories than you use, over time. To gain or keep muscle, keep training and include a protein source at meals (for example eggs, fish, meat, dairy, beans or a simple protein food you already eat). Open the Progress tab to see a personal calorie and protein estimate from your details. Those figures are estimates, not medical advice. A GP or a registered dietitian can help if you want a plan made for you.

    Sleep

    A regular 7–9 hours is a common adult range. Training feels harder when you are short on sleep, so treat rest days and bedtime as part of the plan.

    How the week works

    Each month has a repeating Monday–Friday plan, used for four weeks. Use the weekly rule at the top of the month to progress. Tap Start workout to follow the in-app exercise clips with a timer. The written list is there if you train without the player. Once opened, the app and clips work offline.
